# Daily Glimmer redesign

International English edition for the daily reading product. Palette remains clay-pine: warm paper, pine green, editorial serif for quotes, clean sans serif for controls. The homepage pairs one dated note with a landscape photograph, a small action, and save/copy controls. Past notes and photo credits have dedicated routes.

All 14 sample quotes, themes, actions, image alt text, navigation, feedback messages, metadata, and poster labels are in English. The schedule uses UTC for the international demo. Photo credits retain original author names, source pages, and license links from Wikimedia Commons.
